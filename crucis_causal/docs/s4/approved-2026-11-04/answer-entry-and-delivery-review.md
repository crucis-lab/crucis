# S4 answer entry and delivery evidence

DLV-08, DLV-09. Design review draft, 2026-11-04 Pacific.
This follows the procedure slate and graph, local and paired package
walkthroughs in this review directory.

## Q01 calculation entry

Bind the finite universe U, predicate and mapping, native source and reader,
reference source policy, and evidence frontier. Require a supported interpreted
native answer at this scope, including an explicitly scoped abstention or an
independently interpretable part of an answer. A partial reader identifies
independently committed entries and their evidence. A truncated stream whose
prefix could be revised, or whose framing cannot be checked, does not meet
this requirement. Apply omission only inside a region where the exact native
contract and complete coverage for that rule establish its meaning.

Account for every key in U. An eligible key has a definite interpreted
commitment and an applicable, determinate, uncontested label under the
selected reference policy. An established ineligible key has an evidenced
reason that rules out judgment for this request at this frontier. Examples
include explicit abstention, omission from a fully captured open answer,
and an explicitly indeterminate label in the fully examined selected basis.
An unresolved key could enter or change judgment if missing evidence were
resolved. Preserve the specific dispositions and their evidence alongside
these eligibility decisions.

The covered request expressly targets the established eligible set J at
the named frontier. For positive |J|, return matches over |J|, with J, U,
excluded and unresolved scopes, and the reference and capture bases.
If the reference or reader contract has a global premise that is blocked,
hold the affected calculation even if some apparent matches are visible.
A local premise failure affects only the scope stated by the contract.
An empty J yields an undefined ratio if every key is established
ineligible and calculation entry has otherwise succeeded. If unresolved
keys remain, hold the empty calculation on insufficient evidence. A missing
or unreadable answer holds calculation entry regardless of the size of its
capture inventory. Retain inventory diagnostics on their own scope.
The full-scope request requires every key eligible and positive |U|.
If an explicitly valid empty universe and input meet entry, counts can be
zero and ratios are undefined. Absent evidence of a universe holds entry.

## Q05 delivery, selection and utility

Freeze the intended slots and assigned or received condition basis, eligible
attempts and authorized delivery channels, target recipient or collection
boundary, qualification criterion for an answer, horizon, and selection rule.
For this fixture use the first qualifying delivery at the declared boundary,
by an inclusive horizon H, within eligible attempts. A qualifying delivery
is a complete interpretable answer meeting the task's deliverable contract.
Benchmark quality is assessed separately. Pin the chronological ordering or
tie rule and refuse to invent a winner if selection cannot be established.
Use the delivery event time in a comparable clock or a supported order
relative to H. Ingest and publication time are separate.

Record the delivery finding, selection finding and utility derivation as
different roles. Use exact quality for an established selected qualifying
delivery with established quality. The explicit no-qualifying-delivery
branch assigns zero only if admissible evidence entails that no eligible
qualifying delivery occurred by H. For example, a complete authoritative
history through H accounts for all eligible attempts, channels and events
and classifies all candidate deliveries. An event that might qualify, a
gap, or an unaccounted channel prevents that proof unless independently
excluded. Terminal failure may help establish absence with other evidence.
A zero score on a qualifying delivery is a different basis for zero.

Use a closed range containing the utility of every completion consistent
with the evidence when the slot, target and utility rule are established.
For the fixture the utility rule gives a qualifying delivery benchmark
agreement in [0,1] and a no-qualifying-delivery outcome zero. A total
defined utility contract justifies [0,1] when delivery, winner or score
is unresolved. A narrower range needs evidence that encloses every
remaining branch, including zero if an outcome with no qualifying delivery remains possible.
If the utility definition, applicable slot, or prerequisite for any valid
range is missing, hold the requested enclosure. Incompatible established
premises require assessment rather than a vacuous bound. Report allowed
completion ranges and known facts without converting a range to an observed
outcome.

| Evidence | Utility disposition in the fixture |
|---|---|
| Qualifying 4/5 answer delivered at H, then provider fails | [4/5,4/5] if selected. Horizon is inclusive. Record later failure independently. |
| Provider marks failed, delivery history has a gap | [0,1] on the established utility rule. Whether a qualifying answer arrived remains unresolved. |
| Closed eligible history through H rules out any qualifying receipt, and a qualifying answer arrives later | [0,0] under the no-qualifying-delivery rule. Evidence of a late answer alone leaves earlier eligible deliveries open. |
| Producer reports completed or transport reports accepted for processing | [0,1] if unresolved at the designated delivery boundary and the utility domain is established. Neither report supplies the receipt predicate by itself. |
| Delivery event's possible times straddle H, quality 4/5, and evidence rules out other candidates | [0,4/5] for possible late or timely delivery. The range includes both classifications. |
| Verified timely selected answer has a benchmark quality gap | Retain established delivery. Use [0,1] if the target quality is defined and the only gap is evidence of its value. An ill-defined quality target holds Q05. |
| Early missing candidate could beat known timely 4/5 in the selection order | Preserve the known delivery, hold winner identity, and enclose quality of all feasible winners. No promotion of 4/5 to the selected value. |
| Delivery occurred before H and a receipt arrives after the first evidence freeze | Reassess the original horizon under a new binding. Preserve the earlier assessment at its own evidence frontier. |

For the original paired fixture the first case difference is 3/10 and
K2/A has range [0,1]. If K2/B has established no qualifying delivery
by H, the frame enclosure is [3/20,13/20]. If failure is known but its
eligible delivery history has a gap, K2/B may instead require [0,1].
The enclosure widens to [-7/20,13/20]. These are outer intervals for
the same utility definition under different evidence bindings.
