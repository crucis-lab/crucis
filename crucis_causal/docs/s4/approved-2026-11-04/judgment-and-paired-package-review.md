# S4 package walkthrough: finite judgments and paired utility

DLV-08, DLV-09. Design review draft, 2026-11-04 Pacific.
Symbolic packages and exact hand calculations. The proposed output and profile
scopes are contingent on future qualification and actual calculation receipts.

## Common package boundary

Each package identifies an account and snapshot, observer, source inventory and
availability, audience, and source frontier. IDs below stand for exact frozen
record versions and artifact selectors. The qualification receipt is absent in
these examples. A future computed result names its request, question instance,
binding, procedure version, calculation occurrence, and output role. A held
request carries its applicability or result disposition without inventing a
calculation. Permission and actual source access are distinct from scientific
applicability. Graph and local calculations are P2 candidates. A declared
finite-frame contrast or enclosure adds P3. Inference and reproduction have
separate prerequisites.

## M0. One planned frame, ordinary quality and delivery utility

Sources at Fp. S2 freezes a frame of two cases K1 and K2 and four distinct
assigned slots, one for each case and arm. Arm A minus arm B and equal case
weights are fixed. The native horizon, chosen attempts and selection rule
are pinned. For each case there are ten ordered keys identifying unique
propositions under the same finite binary benchmark task and scale. Within
each case, the benchmark labels for keys 1 through 10 are 1111100000, with
the two case universes separately identified. The intended pairing is by case
and assigned arm. S3 observations supply selected delivery, failure and capture
facts up to the pinned frontier. Benchmark-relative full-scope agreement is
the per-answer quality input. The K1 values below refer to explicit Q01
result roles and their bindings.

| Planned slot | Frozen evidence | Ordinary quality | Delivery utility |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1/A | Closed valid answer, disagrees on keys 2 and 9 only | 8/10 = 4/5 | [4/5,4/5] |
| K1/B | Closed valid answer, disagrees on keys 1 through 5 | 5/10 = 1/2 | [1/2,1/2] |
| K2/A | Capture gap, qualifying native outcome unknown | Unresolved | [0,1] |
| K2/B | Evidenced failure with no qualifying answer by the horizon | No answer quality | [0,0] |

Utility is separately declared for this task. Use full-scope agreement for a
qualifying answer delivered by the horizon and zero for an actual outcome
with no qualifying answer by that horizon. An unresolved observation takes
an enclosure of all utility values consistent with that evidence. The
outcome rule covers both possibilities for K2/A. K2/B's observed failure
and evidence of no qualifying answer together establish singleton utility
zero. An operational failure receipt alone cannot do so if an earlier
qualifying answer could have been delivered.

Q04 asks for the full intended-frame equal-case mean of ordinary quality
differences. K1 contributes 3/10. One of two intended pairs has established
compatible values. Hold the full-frame mean. K2/A is unresolved and K2/B has
no answer to evaluate. The surviving contribution is a case value.

Q05 asks for an outer interval for the full intended-frame equal-case utility
contrast. K2's contrast is in [0,1]. The full contrast is enclosed by
[(3/10+0)/2, (3/10+1)/2] = [3/20,13/20]. It supports a descriptive utility
enclosure for the named assigned slots. Sharpness, sampling uncertainty,
and causal attribution require different evidence. The ordinary quality
request remains held. Reversing the declared contrast direction gives
[-13/20,-3/20] on the same utility basis. Even if every accuracy count is
feasible for K2/A, this ten-proposition utility yields only the grid
{(3+k)/20 : k=0,...,10}, so the enclosure is not the exact attainable set.
If K2/B's qualifying-answer status were also unresolved and its utility
enclosed by [0,1], the same full-frame calculation would give
[-7/20,13/20]. That is a new binding and depends on the second range premise.

## Gaps to close before serialization

1. Pin the requested universe, predicate and directionality, each mapping,
   and the exact scope of the omission rule. Preserve per-key classification
   provenance, including closure-derived negatives and explicit abstentions.
   An absent key needs a reason and its scope.
2. Pin the applicable reference-source policy and the source inventory it
   must examine, including denied or missing required inputs. A determinate
   label within an examined scope and a claim that every relevant conflict
   was ruled out require different evidence.
3. Set the Q01 entry rule for missing, partial and empty interpreted answers.
   Assign stable scientific roles to counts, coverage fractions, agreement,
   and non-value dispositions. Every fraction names its universe or mask.
4. Require the paired frame, assigned or received condition basis, pair and
   slot IDs, selection rule, horizon and attempt lineage. Qualifying scalars
   must have the same measure definition, compatible units and scope, and
   attributable upstream calculation roles. Equal numbers from different
   coverage targets are insufficient.
5. Define utility as a separate outcome-to-value contract. Pin the evidence
   that supports each terminal classification or range, including the
   possibility of delivery before failure and the case of lost observation.
   Record the outer-bound meaning and any unused joint constraints.
6. Close role-level dependencies. The pair diagnostic, full-frame point,
   and utility bound have distinct evidence edges and dispositions. Q05
   can depend on valid per-slot quality values without requiring Q04 to
   succeed. Actual qualification, calculation receipts, audience closure
   and exact reproduction have their own evidence requirements.

Next review exact role cardinalities and the remaining procedure and
reproduction routes. Freeze encoding only after the worked
packages give each required premise, association, value and held disposition
an unambiguous semantic home.
