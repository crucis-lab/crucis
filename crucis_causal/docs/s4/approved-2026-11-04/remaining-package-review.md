# S4 package walkthrough: ordering, forecasts, posterior and reproduction

DLV-08, DLV-09. Design review draft, 2026-11-04 Pacific.
Companion to the procedure slate, judgment and paired walkthrough, and answer
entry and delivery review. This is a semantic review using stipulated sources
and hand calculations. The packages do not supply real qualification receipts.

## Common account

For each package, stipulate an account and immutable snapshot, identified
observer, audience, source inventory and exact frontier. Symbolic names refer
to frozen record versions and artifact selectors. Each formal request selects a
question instance, evidence binding, procedure version and specific output
roles. Applicable premises identify their subject, scope and evidence. The
hypothetical work records below show what a calculation would establish if
observed and qualified. An unavailable prerequisite can be recorded without an
invented calculation. P0/P1 preservation and interpretation may be available
while a particular calculation or inferential role is held.

## O0. Strict ordering against a declared reference

Sources at O0: S2 roster {A,B,C}, strict full-permutation deliverable, and
reference R0 with two keyed constraints r1=A<C and r2=B<C. The reference
expressly states that these are all admissibility conditions. S1 captures
the entire submitted sequence BAC and provides an evidenced one-to-one
mapping from its tokens to roster members. Pin the reader, source and
reference applicability and completeness. The binding and request distinguish
fulfillment, supplied-constraint violations and admissibility.

| Role | Evidence and result |
|---|---|
| Deliverable fulfillment | Position map B=1, A=2, C=3 uses each roster member once. Fulfilled. |
| Supplied constraints | r1 is 2<3 and r2 is 1<3. Zero violations and an empty violated-ID set over {r1,r2}. |
| Admissibility | Admissible under R0's complete criteria. ABC also passes the same criteria. |

Each variant below uses its own binding.
- ACB is a complete deliverable and violates r2. Return one violation and
  inadmissibility even when r2 is only an established necessary condition.
- If R0 is necessary-only, BAC satisfies the supplied rules and full
  admissibility remains unresolved. An explicitly empty complete reference
  admits every valid permutation. An empty necessary-only reference supplies
  zero violations over the empty scope and no full-admissibility basis.
- An authenticated complete submission BA establishes nonfulfillment. A
  capture truncated after BA leaves fulfillment unknown. A native partial
  order is a different representation. Hold the full-sequence violations and
  admissibility for each under this procedure. A cyclic precedence basis is
  outside the first procedure's reference contract and holds applicability.
- Qualification specimens enumerate the six permutations independently and
  distinguish an empty complete reference from missing reference evidence.
  These observations support a named order task, with no causal-direction
  or ranking-score claim.

## B0. One prospective binary probability loss

Sources at B0: S2 task E, whether proposal X passes the designated benchmark
by horizon H, in the fixed case and benchmark version. S1 supplies the exact
native probability 4/5 for E under named information context I. The evidence
establishes issuance at time 10 and outcome availability under the request's
declared information-access policy at time 20 on a comparable timeline.
An applicable benchmark receipt independently establishes E=true. Both
source artifacts, their selectors and provenance, the timeline evidence, and
the selected benchmark disposition are pinned. The request is prospective
single-event loss, with a separate role for the timing assessment.

The matched-event, probability-domain, applicable label and issuance
premises support the calculation. The exact one-coordinate loss is
(4/5-1)^2 = 1/25. This result concerns one forecast and outcome. Calibration
and population performance require their own frame and procedure.

- If a later observation captures a receipt that proves earlier issuance,
  reevaluate prospective eligibility at the new frontier. Capture time and
  issuance time remain separate. If the ordering is unresolved or evidence
  establishes the outcome was already available at issuance, hold the
  prospective request with the corresponding unresolved or contradicted
  premise. An explicitly requested retrospective loss would have its own
  definition and binding.
- A p-value labeled 4/5, a probability for a different benchmark or
  conditioning context, or an unknown or contested outcome holds the loss.
  Preserve the source representation. An established p=0 and y=1 yields
  loss 1 under the same supported premises.
- Qualification tests independently derive the rational loss for both binary
  labels and endpoints, then exercise semantic and chronology refusals
  including overlapping clock ranges with no order evidence.

## F0. Exact posterior in one stipulated finite model

Sources at F0: pinned model M with hypothesis keys H1 and H0, declared
mutually exclusive and exhaustive within M. Priors are 1/2 and 1/2. The
likelihood model gives P(D|H1,M)=4/5 and P(D|H0,M)=1/5 for the same
specified observation event D and protocol. A selected observation receipt
establishes D. The request and basis name M, D, the hypothesis keys and the
roles for joint weights, normalizer and conditional distribution. Distinguish
the model stipulation from the observation receipt.

| Hypothesis | Prior | Likelihood | Joint weight | Posterior |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| H1 | 1/2 | 4/5 | 2/5 | 4/5 |
| H0 | 1/2 | 1/5 | 1/10 | 1/5 |

The normalizer is 1/2 and the posterior sums to one. P2 covers the exact
calculation; P4 covers the posterior conditional on M and D if its premises
are qualified. A multi-case population is not required for this question.
No inference about empirical model fit or causality follows. The 4/5 from
B0 is a separately sourced forecast with a different question and role.

- With both likelihoods established zero, return zero joint weights and
  normalizer, and an undefined conditional posterior under M. Retain the
  evidence that D was observed and the tension with the model. An omitted
  likelihood holds calculation. Unnormalized priors are incompatible input.
  In the initial contract a zero-prior hypothesis still has a declared
  likelihood and a retained key.
- An observation matching a different protocol cannot satisfy the D slot.
  An explicit model assumption permits the stated conditional question while
  supplying no claim that the actual experiment follows that model.
- Qualification independently constructs the finite joint mass table over
  hypothesis and D/complement, then conditions. Include reordered keys,
  missing likelihoods, zero normalizer, and wrong observation protocol.

## X0. Exact computational reproduction of a selected role

Stipulate an original qualified Q01 calculation of the graph package, with
covered agreement 2/2, matched count 2, denominator 2, and judgment mask
{AB,BC}. The frozen original basis names the exact question instance,
selected inputs and selectors, source frontier, masks used as inputs where
applicable, procedure and configuration, exact numeric domain, implementation
and environment pins, and the original observed occurrence and result. The
reproduction request selects that original calculation and role. The audience
has verified access to the required original inputs and applicable comparator
and execution permissions. A fresh occurrence records actual recomputation
from the same frozen basis. This replay recomputes the judgment mask from the pinned sources and rule.
A calculation that takes a frozen mask as an input can reproduce scoring
conditional on that mask. Its scope omits derivation of the mask.
The comparator checks the corresponding role,
scope, derived judgment mask, counts and exact rational value.

| Replay situation | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Same pinned basis, new occurrence, same mask and counts, rational result 1 | Exact semantic agreement. The two occurrence IDs remain distinct. |
| Same pinned basis, new occurrence, derived mask {AB} and ratio 1 | Completed disagreement on the derived result and judgment scope. Equal scalars cannot establish exact agreement. |
| Replay substitutes a different reference revision or input mask, giving 1 | Basis mismatch. Hold comparison as reproduction of the original. The changed basis can support its own calculation. |
| The old reference bytes are unavailable or inaccessible | Hold reproduction at preflight. A digest or narrower public attestation does not provide the original input. |
| An admitted replay starts but no comparable outcome is observed | Retain the replay occurrence and hold agreement on missing result evidence. |
| Original and replay both give the evidenced undefined ratio from whole-query abstention, under a comparator declared for that diagnostic | Exact agreement on that disposition and its scope. The ratio remains undefined. |

Use semantic exact equality in the declared domain. Rational 2/2 and 1 can
agree as quantities, with counts and original representations preserved.
Matching scientific roles and values does not require byte-identical packages.
The original itself needs an attributable observed calculation for this P5
mode. Reproduce an old binding using its own evidence, even if a later
reference revision exists. Exact agreement supports a computational claim
only within this scope. It establishes neither reference correctness nor a
fresh native trial. Qualification includes exact matches, differing results
on the same basis, swapped original inputs, inaccessible originals, and
matching diagnostics whose reasons must also agree.

## Gaps before serialization

1. Type ordering fulfillment, supplied-condition violations and admissibility
   separately. Represent completeness of the submitted artifact separately
   from completeness of the reference criteria.
2. Specify forecast issuance and outcome availability evidence relative to
   the named information-access policy. State the required ordering and
   retain unresolved timing. Bind event, condition and observation versions.
3. Type the finite model partition, hypothesis-to-prior/likelihood association
   and observation protocol. Preserve defined weight/normalizer diagnostics
   independently of an undefined or held posterior.
4. Separate reproduction input-basis matching from output comparison.
   Specify semantic comparator scope, exact equality and diagnostic
   comparator rules. Pin audience access and original/replay occurrence
   evidence. Give basis mismatch, missing evidence, completed disagreement
   and agreement distinct dispositions.
5. Decide exact role cardinalities and reference forms. Freeze neither
   representation nor package-wide profile labels until each claim can
   identify its own scope and evidence dependencies.
