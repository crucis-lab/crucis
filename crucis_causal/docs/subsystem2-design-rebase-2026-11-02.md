## 1. Confirmed direction and proposed detail

Uzair has repeatedly endorsed the direction and continued the design discussion.
The governing requirements are generic, system-independent core mechanisms,
faithful native evidence/meaning, explicit unknowns, scientific rigor, traceable
justification and meaningful coverage. Convenience does not override those goals.

The seven areas below have received first design treatments with examples and
alternatives. Their detailed obligation tables, policies, fields and procedure
choices are proposals until joint review/acceptance. Prose examples, algebra and
documentation checks are not scientific findings, native support or implemented
S2 validation. There is no adopted S2 wire schema or API.

## 3. Remaining path to final design review

1. Review the composed A-H definitions, J01-J12/K01-K06 closure directions and the
   proposed family/profile/lifecycle contract. All five requested extension groups
   now have logical definitions. Resolve exact semantic/compatibility obligations
   before claiming them complete; fictional notation is not real evidence or a
   production wire instance.
2. Audit identity/reference closure, task/condition/transform compatibility,
   scientific versus operational readiness, binding/exposure timelines and analysis
   population consistency. Record unresolved points and decisions explicitly.
3. Settle exact semantic families, requirement profiles and lifecycle rules that
   those examples require. Map coverage to the existing capability axes/families;
   preserve unsupported extensions and native/evaluation limits.
4. Review the closure catalog/services and candidate A-H wire/API/adverse cases.
   Detected representation/interface incompatibilities have proposed resolutions;
   unsupported scientific/native/owner prerequisites have explicit withheld claims.
   No finite specimen set proves universal compatibility; no S2 library is built.
5. Review the prepared final S2 candidate and nine-bundle DAG, including must-show
   criteria, preservation gates and scientific claim limits. Record acceptance and
   input hashes. Implementation begins only with a subsequent explicit go for that
   approved scope; no bundle has started.

This is an iterative sequence: later examples can refine earlier proposals. Do not
freeze convenient field names before the corresponding meanings and failure cases
are settled. Concrete statistic/protocol changes still require their owning
subsystems and W5 adoption; S2 does not copy shared scoring logic.
