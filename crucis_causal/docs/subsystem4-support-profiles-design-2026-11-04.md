# Subsystem 4: proposed support profiles and evidence obligations

Tickets: DLV-08, DLV-09; related DLV-10.
Date: 2026-11-04, America/Los_Angeles.
Status: recommended design selection for Uzair's review. No implementation,
registered profile identifiers, serialization freeze, scientific procedure adoption,
native campaign, paper change or public protocol adoption occurs in this pass.

## 1. Continuation and acceptance history

Uzair endorsed the [measurement capability map](subsystem4-measurement-capability-map-2026-11-04.md)
as the basis for selecting profiles and requested each profile's guarantees,
minimum/conditional evidence and withholding behavior, with concrete examples.
The earlier [opening structure](subsystem4-structure-design-2026-11-04.md) and
question/applicability discussion remain the conceptual foundation. This records
that continuation; it does not retrospectively freeze their precise field forms.
The six-profile choice and detailed obligations below are recommendations for
adjudication, not a claim that the user has already accepted every new rule.

Select six composable S4 guarantees: account preservation; scoped interpretation
and binding; calculation; population/contrast assessment; inference; calculation
reproduction. Keep native validation and other qualification evidence on a separate
axis. P0-P5 below are local discussion handles, not registered wire identifiers.
No profile turns metadata, an adapter name or a numerical result into scientific
correctness. No profile grants execution, disclosure or access authority.

## 2. A scoped guarantee, not one success ladder

A profile claim names its question/binding/subjects, semantic family and features,
reference role, procedure/version/implementation, actual evidence cutoff, intended
scope, audience and limits. Its qualification receipt must cover that combination.
Advertising a capability, having code available, completing an operation, satisfying
a profile on one binding and scientifically validating a native system are separate.

An applicability assessment can complete and faithfully identify a failed or
unresolved prerequisite while a requested calculation profile remains withheld.
Likewise, a negative conformance result or a low quality value can be a successful
measurement. Do not exclude a prediction merely because it is wrong.

Profile satisfaction is scoped: a known adjacency quantity may be established
while orientation or unaligned propositions remain withheld. Do not flatten those
subjects into a partial global pass. Preserve requirements and evidence, including
conflicts and unassessed requirements. Not requested, not yet assessed, unsupported,
unresolved, contradicted, mathematically undefined and operationally denied must
remain distinguishable; exact result forms/enums are still open.

## 3. Recommended profile matrix

Minimum means evidence needed for the stated guarantee, not a requirement that all
native history be known. Unknowns can satisfy honest accounting obligations while
withholding dependent interpretation/calculation. Conditional requirements apply
only when the requested scientific quantity or actual operation needs them.

| Handle / profile | Guarantee at the named scope | Minimum evidence | Conditional evidence | What remains withheld |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 Account preservation | Preserve the supplied measurement intent, evidence declarations, observations, results or gaps with their actual provenance and availability | Identified account/observer and subjects; versioned envelope; exact supplied content/reference identities; source basis, cutoff and explicit known/unresolved scope; integrity for bytes actually claimed captured | Artifacts/digests/selectors, import occurrence, prior-account links, restrictions and audience closure where publication uses them | Missing bytes as captured; original authorship/history from a new import; interpreted native meaning, calculation, scientific truth or execution permission |
| P1 Scoped interpretation and binding | Establish the named meaning of a question and selected evidence, with an explicit applicability account and unresolved prerequisites | P0 for relevant scope; supported exact semantic readers; question/quantity/predicate; target domains and units; identified selected output/reference roles and conventions; binding to actual supplied sources | Mappings/derived views, coverage/omission, candidate/tie/weight/event/model meanings, population/selection definitions and relevant reference requirements | Unknown critical meaning; ambiguous alignment resolved by convenience; satisfaction of unresolved calculation prerequisites; a scientific metric from metadata binding alone |
| P2 Calculation | Execute the identified procedure for a well-defined scoped quantity, preserving inputs, numerical conventions, contributions/result and limitations | P1 for required meanings; exact supported evaluator/configuration and scoped qualification; established prerequisites for this requested quantity; source pins, calculation identity, units/denominators and numerical domain; actual calculation evidence | Reference labels/constraints/distributions if used; derived-view and alignment rules; randomness/approximation/rounding; interval/set/bound rules; authorized S3 execution for effects | Whole-domain accuracy from partial judgment; invented values for absent evidence; truth of a reference/model; population generalization, inferential validity or a later finding threshold by arithmetic alone |
| P3 Population and contrast assessment | Compute the declared estimator/functional for the named frame or comparison, with all intended units and dispositions accounted for | P2 for the actual calculation plus interpreted frame/target, unit identities, contributions or joint-input association, selection, weights, missingness, denominator and aggregation/contrast definition | Matching, nesting/overlap, assignment versus received condition, conformance/deviations, grouping, reference transport, resource overlap, adaptive selection and explicit missing-outcome estimator/bound premises | Wider-population claims from an available-file intersection; attempts/draws as independent trials; unqualified causal attribution of a contrast; full-frame values from undisclosed dropped cases |
| P4 Inference | Apply a specified inferential procedure to its stated target under explicit model/design conditions, with scoped uncertainty and premise support | P2 for computation; exact inferential target/procedure; required sampling/model/design evidence; assumption account and its basis; identified uncertainty sources and inferential limits | P3 where population/contrast reasoning requires it; dependence, selection/tuning/stopping, missingness, multiplicity, model/prior/identification conditions and evaluator approximation as required by the method | A native confidence field as validated inference; all assumptions proved by declarations; empirical validity outside stated conditions; every uncertainty source absorbed into one interval; finding adoption |
| P5 Calculation reproduction | Recompute the specified calculation from its pinned basis and establish the named exact or numerical agreement claim | P2 computational scope; original inputs and result available to the verifier; complete relevant code/procedure/configuration pins, actual access, environment/numerical/randomness information, fixed comparison rule and recomputation evidence | P3/P4 evidence for claims about those original calculations; exact bytes versus quantities; declared tolerance; controlled stochastic replay/comparison only under an explicitly qualified contract | Correct science because the same code repeats; independent scientific replication; native rerun authorization; raw reproducibility from hashes or attestations alone; widened tolerance merely to pass |

P2 is not restricted to per-unit scalar scores. A joint functional, vector, set,
bound or diagnostic can qualify at its own scope. P3 adds the population/comparison
account where that is claimed; it does not require every estimator to average
independent scalar rows. P4 is not forced through a multi-case aggregate when its
target is a single-case model-conditional quantity. Its own relevant design and
population premises still have to be explicit.

P5 is a side branch from a calculation, not the top of a truth ladder. The same
bug or wrong reference can reproduce exactly. Native execution with fresh data is
a separate authorized S3 experiment, not implicit in P5. This distinction is
consistent with the computational reproducibility versus new-data replicability
definitions in the [National Academies report (2019)](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/25303/chapter/3).
No stochastic reproduction mode or tolerance is selected by this proposal.

## 4. Common minimum and conditional obligations

All S4 accounts preserve identified intent/source, author/observer basis, actual
availability, scope/cutoff, versions and gaps. For higher guarantees, identify the
question definition, evidence binding, calculation/assessment occurrence and
their relationships rather than conflating them. References may supply evidence
instead of copying it. Availability or an observed digest is not execution permission.

Add obligations when their meaning is needed:

- A reference-dependent quantity needs the actual reference role, coverage,
  applicability and conflict/uncertainty treatment. Reference-free descriptive
  questions do not require invented truth or a dummy reference.
- Comparisons across domains need supported identity/measurement mappings and any
  allowed ambiguity treatment. Full mappings are required only for claims needing
  them; a scoped invariant or justified bound has its own prerequisites.
- Population claims need the intended frame, multiplicity, weights and dispositions.
  Historical observed-set questions are permitted with that explicit target; they
  cannot acquire an unknown original trial frame by inference from filenames.
- Contrasts distinguish assignment, actual received condition, deviations and
  the requested estimand. Intention-to-treat and per-protocol targets are different
  questions; recording nonconformance is not a universal instruction to exclude it.
- Probability/collection/interval questions need exact event, generation, weight,
  conditioning and construction meanings. A numerical range does not supply them.
- Effects, model queries, private scoring or native calls need their actual S3
  authority, operation contract, effect observations and publication audience.
- Inferential claims need method-specific design/premise evidence. A seed, cluster
  label, native posterior claim or registry entry is insufficient on its own.
- Reproduction needs accessible actual bytes and relevant numerical/random state;
  publication can retain a restricted result without giving every audience P5.

These are semantic obligations, not a frozen required-field list or schema. Lower
profiles may retain unknowns; higher profiles may be withheld only at their affected
scope. No silent deletion, filling, normalization, repair, best-case selection or
question substitution is allowed to manufacture a stronger guarantee.

## 5. Assumptions and conditional scientific claims

Record a premise's role and basis: directly evidenced, design/model-stipulated,
explicitly assumed, unresolved or contradicted, with conflicting evidence retained.
These are conceptual distinctions, not new exact serialized variants. Declared
assumptions are not empirical proof, but an explicitly model-conditional question
can be scientifically meaningful without proving its model universally true.
See [Pearl (2009)](https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~kaoru/SS-2009-57-Sup.pdf) on the conditional
nature and assumptions of causal/counterfactual claims. The profile taxonomy is
our engineering proposal, not a taxonomy adopted from that source.

For example, a posterior summary conditional on a stated model/prior requires that
model, procedure and relevant approximation evidence. It does not establish that
the model describes the world. An inference about an actual experiment cannot
ignore known overlap and then claim independence was established. An explicitly
hypothetical calculation under a different premise has a separate question/binding;
it does not repair the original factual requirement or upgrade its guarantee.

The requested guarantee determines which premises require factual evidence and
which are explicitly conditions of the question. Unknown applicability must not
be laundered through a new assumption. Exact premise grammars, adjudication rules
and qualified inferential methods remain for detailed contract review.

## 6. Family overlay: apply the matrix without narrowing the architecture

All M01-M17 rows of the capability map remain in architectural scope. Select the
common P0-P5 contract machinery across them; qualify individual semantic/evaluator
combinations explicitly. This does not promise that every possible scientific
procedure ships in the first build. The actual built-in procedure inventory,
mathematical definitions and qualification gates must be closed before the build
DAG. No graph-only default defines the framework or answers that open choice.

| Family rows | P0/P1 scope | Additional P2/P3 requirements | Additional P4/P5 limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| F01 M01-M02 structures/classes | Preserve native endpoints, coverage and class meaning; no arbitrary representative | Exact target predicate, reference view, mapping and set/invariant/bound semantics | Dependence/selection and actual target assumptions for inference; reproduce chosen evaluator, not native discovery |
| F02 M03-M04 local/order/ranking | Candidate/query and completeness/tie/precedence meanings | Separate coverage/fulfillment/quality; declared admissibility, relevance and top-k rules | No inference from one admissible answer; retain query/group design and reference completeness |
| F03 M05 numeric parameters/effects | Quantity, domain, units, scale and conditioning | Compatible target/parameterization and explicit conversions; no shape-only agreement | Model/identification and reference uncertainty where needed; numerical mode pinned for P5 |
| F04 M06-M08 probabilities/tests/intervals | Event/hypothesis/construction meanings | Valid scoped numerical procedure and outcome/reference; calibration/coverage populations where requested | Per-event score or interval width is not calibrated/inferential validity; preserve selection and construction premises |
| F05 M09 collections | Member identity/multiplicity, associations and generation/weights | Supported empirical/distribution target, completeness and normalization rule | Draws are not scientific repetitions; inferential/Monte Carlo conditions and replay state are separate |
| F06 M10-M11 models/causal operations | Artifact and operation/domain/target meanings, actual availability | Callable/query evidence and compatible causal/functional reference; effects admitted through S3 | Observational prediction does not certify intervention/counterfactual validity; native re-execution is not P5 |
| F07 M12 learned domains | Preserve maps/alternatives and allowed equivalences | Source-aligned quantity needs supported alignment; invariants/ranges need explicit procedures | No post-outcome alignment presented as independent validation; no automatic cross-domain generalization |
| F08 M13 text/explanations | Preserve text and selected interpreted claims/rubric | Qualified reader/assessor, exact sources, declared score and limitations | Plausibility/source mention does not prove causal reasoning faithfulness; assessor/design uncertainty explicit |
| F09 M14 adaptive interaction | Opportunity/action/delivery and information history | Defined utility/trajectory, initial conditions, actual effects and resource boundaries | Adaptive inference needs its own method; unchosen actions need a counterfactual basis; no identical-final-data assumption |
| F10 M15 operational/resource | Observed facets, units/basis and accounting scope | Nonoverlap/revisions/missingness and declared population or tradeoff functional | No use from availability, cost zero from missingness or native stop from closed collection; no universal budget scalar |
| F11 M16 partial/failure/abstention | Preserve distinctions between native decisions, delivery and capture failure | Only supported components or a declared task-loss quantity; all intended units retained | Missingness assumptions and scoped uncertainty; no complete-result quality or universal zero by default |
| F12 M17 opaque/extensions | P0 source account; P1 only supported metadata or installed exact reader meanings | Independently understood observable properties can qualify; scientific content remains withheld without a reader/evaluator | Operational inference/reproduction can qualify at its own scope; opaque content never gains those claims from metadata |

Each row also inherits relevant X01-X14 modifiers. Temporal, relational, cyclic,
distributed, learned, probabilistic and adaptive combinations require shared-boundary
qualification. Supporting the pieces is not evidence that the combination works.

## 7. Qualification is a separate evidence matrix

Every advertised profile/family/procedure/mode claim must identify its actual
implementation and receipts, tested inputs/designs, native system/version/config
where applicable, platform/environment, audience and limitations. Distinguish:

| Evidence kind | What it can qualify | What it cannot automatically qualify |
|---|---|---|
| Design specification | Intended obligations and examples | Implemented or tested support |
| Synthetic conformance cases | Exact tested contracts, refusals and mechanisms | Native scientific performance |
| Independently specified mathematical cases/reference checks | The scoped evaluator's tested numerical/semantic behavior | Every input, inferential assumption or causal target |
| Recorded-data replay/regression | The selected preserved evidence/compatibility route | Fresh native integration, original chronology or independent trials |
| Controlled native mechanism fixture | The actual exercised invocation/recovery/evidence boundary | A real causal system's scientific accuracy or universal transport support |
| Actual native-system scientific campaign | The exact system/configuration/data/design and measured claims | Other configurations, families, environments or universal correctness |
| Recalculation/independent scientific replication | Respectively the pinned computation or the actual new study under its protocol | Each other's claims or generalizability by default |

These are scoped evidence categories, not one ordinal badge. An installed adapter,
registry entry, successful command, broad family label or package test suite is
insufficient. Record support as proposed/implemented/qualified with actual receipts;
do not turn a data/dependency skip into a pass. Access may allow P2-P4 in a restricted
audience while withholding P5 for a public audience. An attestation remains an
attestation, not public raw-input verification.

## 8. Concrete profile examples

**E01 Partial graph reference.** Three requested adjacency pairs have reference
labels present/absent/undetermined; the native closed answer predicts present/
absent/present. P0 preserves them, P1 interprets the scopes. An explicitly defined
covered-label agreement can qualify for P2 at two correct judgments out of two,
with reference coverage two of three. P3 may aggregate that named quantity across
an accounted population. Full-graph precision remains withheld. Neither P4 nor
P5 follows automatically. This is illustrative arithmetic, not an adopted metric.

**E02 Ten intended cases, eight available quality values.** P2 can compute a mean
of those eight supplied values under that explicit question. P3 must retain all
ten intended units and cannot call the eight-case result the complete ten-case
quality mean. A separately defined delivery-and-quality utility may assign a
specified loss to observed delivery failure; unknown capture loss is not silently
relabeled as that failure. Applicable missing-outcome bounds/estimators need their
own declared procedure and premises. Inference needs the actual design/missingness
account. A paired contrast cannot silently use only the common observed keys.

**E03 Local abstention and order alternatives.** A parent query's whole-query native
abstention with no definite answers can support fulfillment/coverage calculation
while conditional quality has no definite-answer denominator. Do not report it as
perfect or zero conditional accuracy.
For a task admitting ABC and BAC, a full answer BAC can qualify for a declared
admissibility calculation; one partial constraint may be compatible but unfulfilled.
Native semantics, quantity definitions and reference completeness determine P2,
not whether the output resembles the preferred graph or sequence.

**E04 Probability contribution, calibration and uncertainty.** A known probability
for a specified event and an applicable realized label may support P2 under a
qualified outcome-scoring procedure. A population calibration quantity additionally
needs the P3 frame/functional and relevant outcomes. A confidence interval or test
about that population quantity needs P4's applicable design/procedure. A native
confidence field, interval or posterior label alone supplies none of those upgrades.
No scoring formula or calibration method is selected by this example.

**E05 Model-conditional inference with observed dependence.** A defined posterior
quantity may qualify conditionally on its explicit model/prior and numerical
procedure. That does not prove real-world model validity. Overlapping windows in
an actual experiment cannot acquire an iid inference claim by relabeling them
independent. A mathematically hypothetical iid calculation must be identified as
such; inference about the actual design remains separately assessed/withheld.

**E06 Restricted reference and later correction.** An authorized restricted
calculation may qualify for P2/P3/P4 at its audience. A public reader without inputs
does not receive P5 from a digest or attestation. If a reference is later corrected,
publish a new binding/assessment/calculation with the correction relation. The old
calculation may still reproduce exactly against its old reference. That P5 result
does not establish that the old reference was correct.

**E07 Temporal learned ensemble and interrupted native run.** Known native-domain
quantities may qualify while missing source alignment withholds source-edge quality.
Member association, temporal coordinates and probability meanings combine rather
than replace one another. Partial collection can support a predeclared cutoff
quantity without establishing final quality, final cost or native termination.
Recomputing the captured calculation is distinct from restarting the native run.

## 9. Alternatives, reuse and remaining choices

| Choice | Recommended direction | Strongest alternative | Cost and failure prevented |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support organization | Six scoped composable guarantees plus separate qualification evidence | One valid/success ladder ending in native validation | More explicit dependency/claim bookkeeping; prevents metadata or replay being promoted to causal/native validity |
| Population scope | Separate numerical calculation from accounted frame/contrast | Every list mean becomes a population result | More frame/disposition work; prevents silent survivor/intersection analysis |
| Conditional inference | Explicit model/design conditions and evidence limits | Require proof of every premise, or accept declarations as proof | More precise questions and held claims; preserves useful conditional science without inventing factual validation |
| Reproduction | Separate pinned-calculation agreement from scientific replication | One reproducible badge for hashes, replay and new studies | More exact modes and receipts; prevents repeatable errors or restricted attestations from becoming universal verification |
| Broad family scope | Common P0-P5 machinery, exact qualified procedures per combination | Built-in universal graph score or adapter-specific grades | More semantic/evaluator qualification; preserves breadth and native meaning with lighter adapters |

Reuse [S1 implemented support](subsystem1-implemented-support-2026-11-01.md) for
artifacts/domains/output meaning, [S2 implemented scope](subsystem2-implemented-scope-2026-11-02.md)
and [evaluation bindings](../experiments/evaluation.py) for scientific intent,

measurement descriptor remains metadata-only. None of these foundations implements
P0-P5 as S4 profiles or qualifies a new scientific evaluator by itself.

stored results, certificates, research helpers, paper, tracker or site changes occur.
Existing package checks are compatibility evidence, not qualification of these
unbuilt profiles. Private operational correspondence remains outside this document.
