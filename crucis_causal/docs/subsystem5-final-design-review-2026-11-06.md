## Contract at the handoff

An S5 finding is an attributed assessment of one exact question under
one exact rule, on one selected evidence basis at a named cutoff and
audience. The question names its scientific target. The rule names
its decision policy. Applicability, realized execution, sufficiency,
decision and registration remain separately addressable. A source
capture may preserve a legacy statement below the scoped-assessment
floor. S5 hands S6 an authorized selection and its verification limits.

The [contract outline](subsystem5-contract-outline-2026-11-06.md)
defines six scoped support profiles. The
[wire proposal](subsystem5-cardinality-wire-design-2026-11-06.md)
sets record and reference forms, candidate limits and missingness.
The [operations proposal](subsystem5-operations-recovery-legacy-design-2026-11-06.md)
defines effect and authority boundaries. This review closes the
following implementation-facing choices.

- **Preparation and decision sufficiency.** Preparation may establish
  a hold from a missing shared prerequisite. A locally missing operand
  may reach evaluation as an explicit unresolved input when its rule
  permits decisive partial evidence. A qualified branch can then decide
  within that scope. A shared evidence conflict still holds any
  dependent clause.
- **Decisions and observed work.** An evaluated result, including a
  rule-defined inconclusive decision, links actual work and clause
  evidence. A preflight hold needs an attributed basis without an
  evaluator occurrence. A status-only successor can reference the
  exact recorded decision while adding its separate registration basis.
- **Identity and retries.** Freeze the prepared basis and the actual
  limits before admission. Record immutable deltas. Recovery checks
  exact transaction or publication bindings. A changed head requires
  explicit reprepare and a different commit binding. A changed
  scientific basis requires a new assessment. A restarted calculation
  gets a new occurrence, linked where appropriate.
- **Public and private proof.** Use a separately authorized outward
  view and an S3 carrier. Check both closure layers. A dated attributed
  statement can disclose less than the private assessment and support
  a narrower verification claim. A pointer is a separate authorized
  effect.

## First support scope

The initial core is system-agnostic and uses explicitly installed
rules and evidence readers. Scope is qualified per rule family and
evidence combination. Start with exact point comparisons, one-sided
sampling-endpoint and explicit two-branch sampling rules, closed outer
bound comparisons, exact or bounded unit counts, and bounded AND/OR
over qualified clauses. Use exact operator and equality behavior.
Read qualified sampling evidence where available. Estimation and
coverage validation belong to S4 or an explicitly qualified supplied
procedure. The sampling fixture exercises decision semantics and
does not qualify a new interval estimator.

The controlled paths include one local S3 journal and storage route,
actual S1–S4 typed readers for selected scopes, the four cases above,
and the six support profiles at their declared evidence boundaries.
Qualified operational evidence may support an incident rule directly.
The worked R1, bound, compound and incident rules are fixtures for
the generic interface. Existing K01–K15 meaning remains on the
historical route. A new adoption, threshold change, legacy rule
rewrite, or stronger registration claim requires separate evidence
and review.

The first integrated inspection and preparation route uses explicit
local inputs and performs no evaluation or effects. Calculation,
recording and disclosure stay on the typed authority paths. S6 report
rendering and certification, S7 scientific validation of the auditor,
new native campaigns and sealed data access are separate work.

## Gate discipline

Every bundle needs its scoped acceptance and adverse recovery evidence,
an explicit owned-path review, the repository's staged-tree and enabled
hook gates, and a local commit. The final integration records a
claim-to-test map, data availability, and independent numerical oracles
for the qualified rule families. Treat fixture qualification, packaging,
and historical compatibility as separate claims.

Historical comparison stays at the existing 0.001 tolerance. Recheck
the protected historical and checkpoint pins, run the recorded
comparison and existing verifier with the declared data, and record
actual availability and results separately from offline tests. The
[S4 historical receipt](s4/b10-legacy-implementation.md) provides the
prior expected 1,298 comparisons and 14 stored plus 14 freshly computed
certificate checks. Diagnose any change to its source. An unavailable
required historical gate leaves release qualification open.
